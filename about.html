Jill Maxwell

Jill Maxwell has been recognized in the Design Industry for over 15 Years.

Educated at Queen’s University with a Bachelor’s Degree in History and trained in Toronto at The International Academy of Design, Jill founded her Design firm in 2003. Her portfolio includes residential and commercial projects in Toronto, Mississauga, Kleinberg, Creemore, Collingwood and Port Severn.

Rather than showcase a “Signature Style”, Jill and her team embrace each new project as a blank canvas, creating exceptional, timeless and purely unique interiors that are an extension of each client.

Listening to her clients and explicating their needs allows Jill to develop not only an architectural platform in which to provide an exceptional interior but also provides a mechanism to further enhance a “Lifestyle”. Forever trying to juxtapose old with new, remodel old furniture, reuse vintage architectural finds, refurbish barn rescues and restore items slated for landfill, Jill believes Good Design can be interpreted in any medium.

A true Design visionary, Jill focuses on forming collaborations between Architects and Builders, employing carefully skilled talent and sourcing sustainable and local materials to create extraordinary effects and frame lasting lifestyles for her clients.

Embracing the true principles of design, Jill is accomplished in many styles, including: Traditional, Transitional, Contemporary, Muskoka Cottage, UrbanFarm and Country Vernacular. Jill further challenged herself to create five renditions for a Modern, State of the Art Airplane Hanger at the Oshawa Airport in 2017.

Jill Maxwell Design Inc.

Offers a full array of services encompassing: Interior Design, Renovations, New Build Construction, Decorating, Remodelling, Staging and Event Planning. Jill’s design work and advice on renovating solutions have been featured in Style At Home Magazine and The Toronto Star.
