Oak Bay Golf Club

Honey Harbour, Port Severn.
The Remodel of this Golf Club was completed over a three-year period. Initially, the Clubhouse was transformed with more Contemporary elements. The Washrooms, Kitchen, Pizza Oven and Prep Counter, Dining Room, Bar and Lounge and Entrance Foyer were all remodeled and updated for Member usage. A Main Floor Snack Shop was then designed and built as well as an Outdoor Pool enclave complete with Pool Cabana Bar/Snack shop, Outdoor Shower and Pool Changerooms. Complete Landscaping around the Clubhouse was also conceived and tailored to complete the project.
